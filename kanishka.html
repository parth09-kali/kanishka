Our Philosophy

Celebrating the Pinnacle
of Pan-Indian Cuisine

"My aim is to achieve the highest possible quality, not just with the food but in every aspect of the dining experience."

— Atul Kochhar, Executive Chef & Founder

Kanishka celebrates the food culture, cooking styles and signature dishes discovered across India's vast continent — a cornucopia of taste, flavour and texture. Helmed by twice Michelin-starred chef Atul Kochhar alongside Executive Chef Ashok Kumar, we showcase each regional variety of India's distinct cuisines with an inimitable contemporary touch.

Located in the heart of Mayfair's Maddox Street, minutes from Bond Street and Regent Street — our restaurant is a destination for those who believe the finest dining transcends borders.
